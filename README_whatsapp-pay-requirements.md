# WhatsApp Pay: Feature Requirements

**Business analysis course project (Simplilearn CBAP programme, Course-end Project 3).**
This is a case study. It is not a real client project, and it is not connected to Meta or WhatsApp.

## The question
How can people send money inside a chat, without switching to a separate banking app?

## What I did
I analysed the current way people transfer money, and wrote the requirements for a payment feature inside the messaging app.

## What is in the document
1. Company and project overview, with business objectives
2. Stakeholders (internal and external)
3. Risks
4. SWOT analysis
5. Transition state analysis
6. As-Is process map, and two future-state process maps (payment from a chat, and payment by QR code)
7. Context and use case diagram
8. Main features, and in-scope and out-of-scope items (Phase 1)
9. Requirements (business, functional and non-functional)
10. Wireframes (send money, payments home and history)

## Diagrams
Images are in the `diagrams` folder: As-Is flow, Future flows (chat and QR), use case diagram, SWOT, and two wireframes.

## Skills shown
Stakeholder analysis, risk identification, SWOT, process mapping (AS-IS and TO-BE), use case modelling, requirements writing, wireframing.
